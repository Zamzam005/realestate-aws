About RealEstate

A marketplace built for Somalia

RealEstate connects property owners and vehicle sellers directly with buyers. No middlemen, no agent fees—just direct communication and transparent pricing. Every listing is verified and current, posted by the person who actually holds the keys.

  1. 1

    Post your listing

    Fill in a few details about your property or vehicle, add a photo, and publish. It goes live immediately.

  2. 2

    Receive direct calls

    Your phone number is on your listing. Interested buyers call you directly to ask questions and arrange viewings.

  3. 3

    Close the deal

    Negotiate terms and agree on a price. You control the transaction from start to finish with no commission.
